## Identity
- Public name: First Aid Centre of Sweden (Bangladesh)
- Legal name: Första Hjälpencentrum i Sverige AB (First Aid Center of Sweden Ltd.), a Swedish private company
- Mother branch: Första hjälpencentrum, https://www.forstahjalpencentrum.se
- Founded in Sweden in 1989 (CEO started as an instructor). Bangladesh operation since 2017.
- Current site says "34 years" of experience. From 1989 that is now 37 years.

## What they do
1. **First aid and CPR training** for 8 sectors:
   - General (adult high/low risk, child, infant and toddler)
   - Corporate (banks, insurance, IT, telecom, hotels, multinationals)
   - Construction and industry (garments, power and energy, real estate)
   - Defence and law enforcement (military, police, fire service)
   - Embassies and foreign organisations (NGOs, agencies, embassies)
   - Medical (doctors, nurses, interns, students, midwives, hospital staff, nannies)
   - Academic and sports (schools, colleges, universities, sports academies)
   - Other (airlines, railway, transport)
   - Topics: recovery position, CPR with defibrillation, choking, drowning, bleeding, fractures, burns, poisoning, stroke, heart attack, epilepsy, diabetes, fever, head injury, amputation, heat stroke, shock, penetrating injuries, concussion (SCAT), dehydration.
   - Programme is designed to meet Bangladesh labour-law workplace-safety requirements.
2. **First aid and emergency equipment supply**:
   - AEDs (Defibtech): Lifeline AED, Lifeline VIEW (semi-automatic), Lifeline PRO (manual)
   - Bandage and cleansing: combi bandage, finger bandage, wound-cleansing wipes, elastic bandage, plasters (plastic, children's, textile, clippable), sterile compresses, surgical tape, wound-closure strips
   - CPR kit: CPR mask, face shield, scissors
   - Emergency equipment: traction splint, rescue blanket, vacuum mattress, extrication device, tourniquet
   - First aid products: saline, glucose tablets, burn hydrogel, eyewash, Xylocaine ointment, gloves, tweezers, scissors
